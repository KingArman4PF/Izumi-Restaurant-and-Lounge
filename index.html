Our Story

A Culinary
Oasis in the Heart
of Kololo

"Izumi" is a Japanese word meaning 'Spring' or 'Fountain'. It is believed in many cultures that water has a powerful energy to provide the perfect balance in a place — hence our choice of name.

At Izumi we serve food as we believe it should be. We value our customers and hope to see you leave here each time smiling, only to be thinking when to return.
